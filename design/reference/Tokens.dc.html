Lira · Design system · Tokens

Black-and-white at a glance. Colour is the reward.

Dark only. Every structural element — text, keypad, chips, navigation — is white and greys on a near-black base. Cedar Lichen marks positive moments, a faint cedar glow warms one corner, muted tones identify categories, and red appears only when a limit is actually crossed, always with words.

Typography

Numbers are the hero. Space Grotesk carries every amount — its open, slightly quirky figures give big numbers warmth. Inter carries the interface. Both are set with tabular figures so totals never jitter while they count. Hierarchy comes from size, weight and the three text greys — not boxes.
