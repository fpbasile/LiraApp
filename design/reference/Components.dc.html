Navigation bar

Floating bar, full width. 56dp tall, 20dp from the sides and the bottom edge, no border; content scrolls underneath it. Inactive tabs are just icons; the active tab widens into a filled pill with its label, so where you are is shown by shape and text, not colour. Three tabs, not four. Travel is a mode, not a place: you start a trip from the currency chip (or More › Trips), and while it runs the whole app shifts — Home shows a trip banner that opens the trip view. A Travel tab would sit empty most of the year. Home is always one tap away; tapping it while on Home clears the keypad.

Buttons & fields
Primary buttons are white on black — green is reserved for what happens after you act, not for the button itself.
